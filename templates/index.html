04 / COLOR VISION

Color vision, explained.

Learn how people experience color differently, then try an educational screening.

◉
◉

Color is a conversation between light and your eyes.

Color vision differences affect how some hues are seen or separated. They are common and vary from person to person.

01 · RED SENSITIVITY

Protanopia

Red-sensitive cone cells are absent. Reds may appear dimmer, and some reds and greens can be hard to tell apart.

02 · GREEN SENSITIVITY

Deuteranopia

Green-sensitive cone cells are absent. Some greens, reds, and browns may look similar.

03 · BLUE SENSITIVITY

Tritanopia

Blue-sensitive cone cells are affected. Blues and greens, or yellow and pink, can be more difficult to distinguish.

IN EVERYDAY LIFE

Color differences may affect reading charts, choosing clothing, interpreting status lights, or spotting items on a colored background. Labels, patterns, and good contrast can help.

A SHORT INTERACTIVE ACTIVITY

Try a color vision check.

Look at each original pattern and choose the shape or number you can make out. Results are for learning and reflection.

ⓘ Educational screening only. This is not a medical diagnosis. A professional eye examination is required for proper assessment.
